# Red Horned Everyman — Behavior

Restrained, grounded physical acting.

Use:

- subtle head turns
- eye reactions
- skeptical observation
- dry/deadpan curiosity
- small facial changes instead of broad slapstick
- calm physical presence

His curiosity should read approximately as:

> "I need to understand why this works."

Do not make him constantly furious, villainous, supernatural, manic, or
hyperactive.

Do not give him magical powers by default.

Keep actions physically readable for short-form video.

## Environment policy

Environment policy is completely dynamic.

Infer the opening location and activity per episode from:

- topic + final script + character tone

He has NO permanent:

- home
- city
- room
- lair
- infernal environment
- farm
