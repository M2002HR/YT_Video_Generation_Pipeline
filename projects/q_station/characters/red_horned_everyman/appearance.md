# Red Horned Everyman — Appearance

Adult red-skinned horned humanoid with a slim, lanky build.

Exactly TWO enormous symmetrical black ridged horns growing from the head and
sweeping upward and outward.

- Long pointed ears.
- Long angular nose.
- Heavy, tired, slightly drooping eyes.
- Short tousled black hair.
- Narrow pointed black goatee.
- Two small lower tusks.

## Clothing

- Muted red-and-charcoal plaid overshirt with rolled sleeves.
- Dark charcoal undershirt.
- Faded blue jeans.
- Sturdy worn brown work boots.

## Baseline expression

- serious
- skeptical
- observant
- slightly weary

He is NOT automatically:

- angry
- evil
- goofy
- villainous

## Strongest immutable identity anchors

- exactly two large black ridged horns and their silhouette
- red skin
