# Red Horned Everyman — Negative Constraints

Do not add wings.
Do not add a tail.
Do not add hooves.
Do not add extra horns.
Do not add goat legs.
Do not add armor.
Do not add flames.
Do not add a hell environment because of his appearance.
Do not add glowing eyes.
Do not add a supernatural aura.
Do not turn him into a muscular monster.
Do not make him villainous unless the actual story explicitly requires a villainous performance.
Do not replace the plaid outfit with fantasy clothing.
Do not unintentionally crop both horn tips in a composition intended to show the full head.

## IMPORTANT — labeling

Avoid using the words **demon**, **devil**, **satan**, or equivalent infernal
labels as the PRIMARY character identity prompt. Those labels bias image/video
models toward unwanted supernatural environments and iconography.

## Canonical sheet is IDENTITY_ONLY

The sheet defines:

- face
- silhouette
- horn geometry
- body proportions
- hair
- facial hair
- outfit

The sheet does NOT define:

- scene layout
- turnaround grid
- background
- pose
- camera angle
- environment
