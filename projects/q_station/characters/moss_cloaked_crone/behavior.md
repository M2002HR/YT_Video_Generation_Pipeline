# Performance

Moves with compact, deliberate gestures and a low forward hunch. Her long fingers lead attention before her body does. She observes first, reacts with a knowing side-eye or restrained crooked grin, and guides discoveries without broad theatrical spell-casting. The raven may anticipate, point, watch, hop, or mirror her attention, but never replaces her agency. Keep acting readable and economical; mystery comes from timing and composition, not generic magical spectacle.
