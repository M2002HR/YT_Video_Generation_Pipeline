The fixed show progression is: a varied ordinary situation makes the question visible; the host
reaches or reveals a relevant storybook; the closed topic-styled book opens; the camera enters its
illustrated page; the factual body begins in the topic world. The second narration segment is about
the book retrieval/opening and should take roughly 3 seconds. The narration need not say "book" if
the visual hinge remains unmistakable, but it must support that action.
