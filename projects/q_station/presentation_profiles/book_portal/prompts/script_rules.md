The fixed show progression is: a topic-specific visible discrepancy or consequence makes the question visible; the host
reaches or reveals a relevant storybook; the closed topic-styled book opens; the camera enters its
illustrated page; the factual body begins in the topic world. The second narration segment should take roughly 3 seconds and deliver a concrete clue
while the visual book retrieval/opening proceeds. The narration need not say "book" if
the visual hinge remains unmistakable, but it must support that action.

The spoken entry is not a report of camera movement or prop retrieval. It must advance the
selected question with a supported clue. Preserve the entry mechanics; vary the specific story,
not the identity of the portal. Never give the host unconfigured magical powers or a fixed job.
