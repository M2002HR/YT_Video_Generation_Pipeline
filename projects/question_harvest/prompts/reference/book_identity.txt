Locked identity of the Question Harvest book, and how it must be used.

This file is the single description of the book that appears in every episode. It is read by
the pipeline as the book's identity source, so it describes the object — not one episode's
scene, and not a shot list. Anything episode-specific belongs on the page, never on the book.

==================================================
THE OBJECT
==================================================

One antique storybook, hand-drawn, warm and inviting, clearly old and cared for.

Locked features, every episode, without exception:

- antique brown leather cover, worn softly at the edges and corners
- brass corner protectors on all four corners
- a brass side clasp on the fore-edge
- an eye symbol at the centre of the cover
- a crescent moon above the eye
- small scattered stars around them
- a thick block of aged, uneven pages
- a green ribbon bookmark falling from the pages

Nothing else is added to the cover: no title, no lettering, no numbers, no new symbols.

==================================================
THE BOOK'S OWN LOOK — THE HOUSE STYLE
==================================================

The book always belongs to the series' own world, never to the episode's:

- pure 2D hand-drawn illustration, clean ink outlines, flat colours, simple cel shading
- warm parchment and leather tones, soft even light, gentle paper grain
- handcrafted and natural, the way a good children's encyclopaedia is drawn
- no 3D, no CGI, no photoreal leather or metal, no lens effects, no glow abuse

This is the "main texture": the leather, the brass, the ribbon and the page edges look the
same in every episode, so the book reads as the same object each time.

==================================================
THE PAGES — WHERE THE EPISODE LIVES
==================================================

The episode's subject appears **only inside the illustration on the page**, and only in that
episode's own world style. The page art is a picture inside a book: framed by the page, with
paper margins around it, sitting on the paper rather than replacing it.

- the book, the leather, the brass and the ribbon never take the episode's style
- the page illustration takes the episode's style completely
- the paper itself stays aged paper: margins, grain and a soft inner-spine shadow remain visible

The composited spread the pipeline builds is the authority for where the page art sits; this
description exists so the generated book matches it.

==================================================
WHAT THE BOOK IS NEVER
==================================================

- never held by hands, never carried, never opened by a person
- no people, no characters, no creatures anywhere in a book reference
- never a different book: no new fantasy tome, no redesigned cover, no altered proportions
- never text on the cover or on the visible pages
- never a floating portal, never magical rays filling the frame

==================================================
MOTION, WHEN THE BOOK IS ANIMATED
==================================================

Allowed: slow page turning, gentle paper movement, a soft glow at the page edge, a few
floating specks of dust, and a slow camera push toward the page.

Not allowed: anything inside the page illustration moving, camera orbits, 3D perspective
changes, dramatic depth of field, or a cut that changes the book's design mid-shot.

The page illustration stays a still drawing until the camera has entered it.
