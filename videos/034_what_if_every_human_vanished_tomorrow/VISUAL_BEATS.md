# Visual Beats

### Beat 1

Narration:
At first, your hose might keep flowing from stored water pressure.

Visual:
Cinematic charcoal-and-ink view of an unattended suburban backyard, a garden hose snaking across wet concrete and still spraying a weak arc into an overgrown flowerbed, with the empty house looming behind through gray haze.

### Beat 2

Narration:
Within hours, unattended power grids would begin failing.

Visual:
Wide upward charcoal view of an empty electrical substation at dusk, transformers and insulators receding beneath transmission towers while one isolated cold-amber utility light dims in the storm-gray air.

### Beat 3

Narration:
Many nuclear reactors would shut down automatically.

Visual:
Distant elevated charcoal panorama of a nuclear power station under heavy overcast, cooling towers and containment buildings sitting still as steam output fades into a pale ink-wash sky, with no workers or vehicles present.

### Beat 4

Narration:
Cooling systems would still need power.

Visual:
Simplified non-instructional charcoal cutaway of a reactor building focused on a dark cooling-water loop and pump chamber, with a small reservoir rendered in diluted blue-green ink and the surrounding machinery fading into soot shadow.

### Beat 5

Narration:
Pets trapped indoors would face danger quickly.

Visual:
Intimate charcoal interior of an abandoned apartment kitchen, a house cat standing beside an empty water bowl near a closed door, pale daylight filtering through blinds and casting long soft bars across the floor.

### Beat 6

Narration:
Some farm animals would escape or starve.

Visual:
Windblown charcoal farmyard seen from outside a broken fence, several cattle moving through a collapsed gate toward an empty field while a dark barn and scattered feed troughs sit abandoned in the background.

### Beat 7

Narration:
Without pumps, subways could begin flooding within days.

Visual:
Deep one-point-perspective charcoal view down an empty subway tunnel, dark water already covering the rails and reflecting a few dying amber emergency lamps while stairs disappear into mist behind.

### Beat 8

Narration:
Weeks later, weeds would spread across untended roads.

Visual:
Top-down oblique charcoal streetscape weeks later, cracked asphalt overtaken by tufts of dead moss green weeds pushing through lane seams around a rusting abandoned bicycle and windblown debris.

### Beat 9

Narration:
Years later, weather would weaken empty buildings.

Visual:
Low-angle charcoal view of a weather-beaten apartment block years later, rain streaks and cracks cutting across the facade as a section of roofline sags, vines cling to balconies, and storm haze erases the distant skyline.

### Beat 10

Narration:
Earth would keep going; our systems would not.

Visual:
Quiet panoramic charcoal vista of a reclaimed city edge at dawn-gray light, trees and tall grasses overtaking a silent roadway in the foreground while corroded towers and collapsed utility lines recede into soft ink-wash mist.
