Static wide frame at dawn: dew-dark raised beds and narrow paths, one abandoned hose hissing across an empty allotment, flooding soil into muddy overflow beside a dropped glove and tipped trowel. The crone freezes mid-reach, then her long fingers emerge unmistakably from a moss-green sleeve holding her orb into the spray, clearly directing the moment. In its wet reflection: deserted streets, stalled transit, creeping vegetation, flooded infrastructure, and weathered buildings foreshadow a humanless world.
