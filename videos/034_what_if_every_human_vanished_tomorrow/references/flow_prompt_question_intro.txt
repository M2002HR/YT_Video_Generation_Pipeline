Hand-drawn 2D educational animation, muted natural palette, clean dark contours. At frame zero, the tiny hunched moss-cloaked crone checks a dripping irrigation joint as an unattended hose floods a raised bed beside a dropped glove and tipped trowel. She freezes, follows the spray with a sharp side-eye, glances at her black raven, then draws her orb from beneath her cloak. Her long fingers raise it into the spray; the empty garden reflects on its surface as camera enters the orb.
