## Description — paste into YouTube

Why can a tune keep looping in your head after the music stops? This Short explores involuntary musical imagery, often called an earworm. Recent or repeated listening can trigger it, while short, distinctive fragments may be easier to retrieve. Repetition can make the next note feel predictable, allowing your brain to replay the pattern without external sound. Idle moments, mood, and personal memories can strengthen the cue, while an absorbing task can shift attention until the loop fades.
