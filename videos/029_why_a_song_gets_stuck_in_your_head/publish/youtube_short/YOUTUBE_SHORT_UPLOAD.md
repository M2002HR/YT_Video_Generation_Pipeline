## Description — paste into YouTube

Why can a tune keep looping in your mind after the music stops? This Q Station Short explores involuntary musical imagery, often called an earworm. Recent or repeated listening can trigger a musical fragment, while short, distinctive patterns can be easier to retrieve. Repetition can make the next note feel predictable, and idle moments, mood, or personal memories can give the loop another cue. An absorbing task can shift attention elsewhere until the pattern fades. Q Station is an educational channel where every question opens a world.
