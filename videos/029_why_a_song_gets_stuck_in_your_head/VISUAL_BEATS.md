# Visual Beats

### Beat 1

Narration:
Scientists call it involuntary musical imagery, or an earworm.

Visual:
Vertical screen-print collage of a simplified abstract brain in three-quarter view with a bright looping ribbon of repeated geometric sound-pulses circling through it, dense black contours and cherry-red, violet, and yellow ink shapes on coarse off-white paper.

### Beat 2

Narration:
Recent or repeated listening can trigger it.

Visual:
Red Horned Everyman, rendered as a restrained pop-art screen-print figure, stands beside a generic tabletop speaker while identical colored pulse-shapes repeatedly stream toward his ear; several fading pulse echoes behind him imply recent listening without any notation or text.

### Beat 3

Narration:
Short, distinctive fragments are easier to retrieve.

Visual:
A bold screen-printed memory shelf holds many muted abstract shape-clusters, while one compact hot-pink and lemon-yellow zigzag fragment is being pulled forward by a simplified black hand-shaped attention cue.

### Beat 4

Narration:
Repetition makes the next note feel predictable.

Visual:
A diagonal staircase of repeating red-orange geometric pulse tiles climbs upward, with the final position shown as an empty outlined slot visually implied by the rhythm of the preceding shapes; no musical notation or symbols.

### Beat 5

Narration:
Your brain can replay that pattern without any sound.

Visual:
Red Horned Everyman sits calmly with unplugged headphones resting on a nearby table while a closed loop of vivid geometric pulses visibly circles inside a translucent graphic silhouette of his head, with no pulse connecting to any external source.

### Beat 6

Narration:
Idle moments give the loop room to return.

Visual:
A lone simplified figure waits at a nearly empty bus-stop-like shelter in a wide vertical composition; most of the frame is quiet negative space while a small familiar loop of colored pulse-shapes drifts back into the upper area around the figure's head.

### Beat 7

Narration:
Mood or personal memories can strengthen the cue.

Visual:
An abstract memory collage radiates from a small central pulse fragment: a warm sun-like disk, a simple chair silhouette, a hand-held keepsake shape, and a soft facial-profile silhouette all connected by thick curved black pathways that brighten toward the center.

### Beat 8

Narration:
Fighting it can keep your attention stuck on it.

Visual:
Red Horned Everyman presses both hands against a large springy loop-shaped ribbon as if trying to push it away, but the ribbon bends back around and points toward his face; his expression stays skeptical and restrained rather than frantic.

### Beat 9

Narration:
An absorbing task can pull attention elsewhere.

Visual:
The host leans over a tabletop assembling a complex but readable arrangement of interlocking geometric pieces and workshop tools; his gaze is fixed on the task while the earworm ribbon is displaced to a small, faint corner shape behind him.

### Beat 10

Narration:
Then the loop often fades on its own.

Visual:
A once-bold looping ribbon of geometric pulses crosses a quiet off-white field and progressively breaks into lighter, smaller screen-print marks until only subtle registration traces remain, with the host visible as a small calm silhouette walking away at the bottom.
