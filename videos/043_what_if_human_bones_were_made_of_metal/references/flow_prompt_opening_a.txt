In a quiet engineering display room, the Newton-inspired scholar is already comparing two small body frame models on a table. In the first moments, he gently bends a flexible frame and a shiny metal rod frame side by side: the flexible structure curves naturally while the metal frame barely moves, making the strength-versus-movement mismatch clearly visible. He studies the stiff metal frame with a small pause of surprise, realizing that a harder skeleton may create new limits. He carefully places both models beside the topic-styled storybook. The camera follows the handoff as the scholar leaves the models and a closed storybook sits alone in a clean top-down view. The book opens to reveal an illustrated human-shaped figure with a shiny internal metal frame, transitioning into the topic world.
