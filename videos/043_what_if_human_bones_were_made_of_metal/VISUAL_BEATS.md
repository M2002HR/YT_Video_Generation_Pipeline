# Visual Beats

### Beat 1

Narration:
Metal bones could carry heavy loads.

Visual:
A paper-cut human skeleton with metallic bone sections lifting a large stone weight in a warm anatomical workshop landscape, showing increased carrying capacity.

### Beat 2

Narration:
Joints need movement, not only strength.

Visual:
A close paper-cut view of a knee and elbow joint made from layered bone and soft tissue pieces, bending smoothly beside a rigid metal hinge comparison.

### Beat 3

Narration:
Real bones are strong and slightly bendable.

Visual:
A natural bone structure made from cream paper layers gently flexing like a living branch while maintaining its shape.

### Beat 4

Narration:
Metal frames bend less than bones.

Visual:
A split visual of a flexible bone-like structure curving and a metal frame resisting the same bending force.

### Beat 5

Narration:
The body could become too heavy to move.

Visual:
A full-body silhouette with oversized metallic bones struggling to take a step through soft grass, showing weight affecting movement.

### Beat 6

Narration:
Muscles may struggle with a heavier skeleton.

Visual:
Layered paper muscles around a metallic skeleton pulling with effort as the body attempts to lift an arm.

### Beat 7

Narration:
Metal bones could make repairs harder after damage.

Visual:
A damaged metallic bone section with complicated repair pieces and tools compared with a simpler living bone healing scene.

### Beat 8

Narration:
Medical parts called implants use titanium for support.

Visual:
A realistic paper-cut medical implant piece made of titanium placed beside a human bone, showing a small supportive role.

### Beat 9

Narration:
They do not replace a whole living skeleton.

Visual:
A complete living skeleton with one small titanium implant area highlighted by placement and scale contrast.

### Beat 10

Narration:
A strong skeleton must help the body move and work well.

Visual:
A balanced human skeleton walking and reaching naturally while surrounded by flowing muscle and movement shapes.

### Beat 11

Narration:
Metal gives strength, but it can also cause movement problems.

Visual:
A final balanced comparison showing a strong metal piece beside a flexible living skeleton moving freely.
