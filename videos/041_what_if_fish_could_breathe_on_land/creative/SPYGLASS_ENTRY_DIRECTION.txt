Art-direction brief

Oblique rear-three-quarter view of the captain using a brown-and-brass spyglass beside a coastal research garden pool, with the small eyepiece aligned to his unpatched eye and his blue-coated arm supporting the barrel toward a fish crossing damp ground. The revealed habitat shows amphibious speculation: wet soil, coastal plants, shallow water, and fish exploring land while retaining water-dependent conditions. Realistic textures emphasize biological limits, curiosity, and the captain's careful observational agency.
