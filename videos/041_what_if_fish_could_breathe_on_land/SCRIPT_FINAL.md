If fish could breathe on land, why would they still need water? The answer starts with one change, not a whole new fish. Air breathing solves one problem. The fish still needs water. Its body must handle dry ground. Its fins must support land movement. Water helps many fish survive. Some fish already use air sometimes. Scientists study these fish changes. A land fish needs many more changes. One ability cannot change everything. Adaptation means changes living things make to survive in a place.
