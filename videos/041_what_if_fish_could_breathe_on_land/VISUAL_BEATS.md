# Visual Beats

### Beat 1

Narration:
Air breathing solves one problem.

Visual:
A small fish near a shallow shoreline lifts its head above water and gulps air while bubbles trail behind it.

### Beat 2

Narration:
The fish still needs water.

Visual:
The same type of fish swims between water and a muddy bank, with its wet body partly supported by the shallow pool.

### Beat 3

Narration:
Its body must handle dry ground.

Visual:
A fish rests on damp earth beside a puddle, showing the challenge of supporting its body away from water.

### Beat 4

Narration:
Its fins must support land movement.

Visual:
Close view of a fish using sturdy fin movements against wet sand near the shore, with fin structure clearly visible.

### Beat 5

Narration:
Water helps many fish survive.

Visual:
A colorful group of fish moves through a healthy shallow aquatic habitat with plants, rocks, and flowing water.

### Beat 6

Narration:
Some fish already use air sometimes.

Visual:
A mudskipper-like fish climbs on a wet bank beside mangrove roots while taking air from above the water.

### Beat 7

Narration:
Scientists study these fish changes.

Visual:
A scientist observes a fish in a natural shoreline research setting with simple field tools and a magnifying lens.

### Beat 8

Narration:
A land fish needs many more changes.

Visual:
A visual comparison in the same crayon world shows a fish silhouette with highlighted areas for body support, fins, and moisture needs without any text labels.

### Beat 9

Narration:
One ability cannot change everything.

Visual:
A fish with air-breathing ability returns to the water beside a dry landscape, emphasizing that one feature is not enough for land life.

### Beat 10

Narration:
Adaptation means changes living things make to survive in a place.

Visual:
A vibrant shoreline ecosystem shows different fish and plants fitting their environments, with the adapted fish swimming between water and land.
