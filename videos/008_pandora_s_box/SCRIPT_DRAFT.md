Pandora never actually opened a box. In the oldest Greek version of the myth, she opens a sealed jar, and suffering and hardship escape into the world. She closes it, but too late. Only hope remains inside. Centuries later, the jar became a box in popular retellings, while the warning stayed the same: one tempting choice can release consequences you can’t easily contain. That’s why “opening Pandora’s box” still means starting something that quickly becomes much bigger than you expected.
