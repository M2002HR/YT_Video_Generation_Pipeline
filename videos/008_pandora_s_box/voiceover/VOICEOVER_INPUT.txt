Pandora never actually opened a box. In the oldest Greek version of the myth, she opened a sealed jar—and suffering and hardship escaped into the world. She slammed it shut, but it was too late. Only hope remained inside. Centuries later, retellings turned the jar into a box, but the warning survived: one tempting choice can unleash consequences you can’t easily contain. That’s why “opening Pandora’s box” still means starting something that suddenly becomes far bigger than you expected.
