## Image-generation prompt

```text
Create exactly ONE standalone 16:9 image for one visual beat in a faceless YouTube psychology video.

This is NOT a storyboard sheet, NOT a comic page, NOT a collage, NOT a contact sheet, and NOT a multi-panel layout.
Do not divide the image into panels or frames.
Do not add borders, separators, panel numbers, captions, subtitles, narration text, scene labels, logos, or watermarks.

REFERENCE PRIORITY — follow this hierarchy strictly:
1. CHARACTER ANCHOR is the source of truth for the protagonist's identity: face shape, dark tousled hair, eye/eyebrow design, age impression, green hoodie identity, body proportions, and overall character design.
2. STYLE ANCHOR is the source of truth for rendering: polished 2D illustrated-cartoon look, cinematic but clearly illustrated lighting, soft painterly shading, palette behavior, texture, line treatment, and detail level.
3. Any video-specific reference image supplied for a recurring bedroom, memory, prop, or supporting cast controls that recurring element.
4. PREVIOUS BEAT IMAGE is only for short-range continuity: immediate pose logic, room state, prop placement, lighting progression, and emotional progression.
5. This current prompt controls the new action and composition.

If the previous beat image has drifted from the CHARACTER ANCHOR or STYLE ANCHOR, correct the drift and follow the anchors.

GENERAL IMAGE RULES:
- one dominant visual idea
- clean readable composition
- moderate detail, not hyper-detailed
- strong silhouettes and readable facial emotion
- enough negative space for subtle zoom/pan in editing
- keep critical subjects away from extreme edges
- no photorealism
- no 3D-render look
- no generic corporate-vector look
- no unnecessary text
- any unavoidable story-native text must be extremely short and visually secondary
- preserve visual continuity without copying the previous frame mechanically


CURRENT BEAT — BEAT 05:
Continue the daytime environment from the previous beat, but make the visual metaphor more explicit: the protagonist is at the center while several outside demands pull his attention outward in different directions.

Use subtle directional cues — reaching hands, notification icons, work items, people calling to him, or visual motion lines — all oriented away from the protagonist's head toward the external world. His own head/inner mental space should feel comparatively quiet and visually simple.

Avoid making the image frantic or slapstick. The point is outward-directed attention, not panic.

CONTINUITY REQUIREMENT:
Use Beat 04 only to preserve immediate visual continuity where relevant. Do not simply duplicate it; advance the scene to the new beat.

OUTPUT REQUIREMENT:
Generate exactly one high-quality standalone 16:9 image.
```
