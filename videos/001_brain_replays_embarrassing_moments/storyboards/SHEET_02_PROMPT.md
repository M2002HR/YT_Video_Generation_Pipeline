# Video 001 — Storyboard Sheet 02 Prompt

Covers: Beats 05–08

```text
Create a seamless 2x2 storyboard sheet for a faceless YouTube psychology video.

GLOBAL STYLE:
Preserve the same recurring young male character and polished illustrated-cartoon style established in Sheet 01: dark tousled hair, expressive eyes, youthful proportions, cinematic but illustrated lighting, cohesive storybook rendering. Keep the same recognizable bedroom and overall visual language.

INVISIBLE CROP LAYOUT:
Create one exact 2x2 quadrant composition on a 16:9 full canvas.
All four quadrants must be equal size and aligned to the exact horizontal and vertical center.
There must be NO visible grid, NO separator lines, NO comic frames, NO boxes, NO panel numbers, and NO corner labels.
Treat the center lines as invisible crop boundaries only.
No important character, prop, icon, glow, thought, shadow, or memory element may cross a crop boundary.
Each quadrant must remain a complete standalone 16:9 composition after a simple 50/50 crop.

TEXT POLICY:
Do not add captions, narration text, subtitles, labels, scene numbers, logos, or watermarks.
Small story-native text is allowed only if naturally part of the scene, such as a digital clock display. Prefer no text.

TOP LEFT — BEAT 05:
Daytime. Show the same recurring character being pulled outward by competing demands: phone notification icons, work papers, a laptop, nearby people, and general activity. The key idea is attention being pulled outside his own head. Keep the composition busy but readable.

TOP RIGHT — BEAT 06:
Transition back to night. Show the same character in the bedroom while the daytime distractions visually fade away, dissolve, or drift into darkness. The room becomes quieter and less visually crowded.

BOTTOM LEFT — BEAT 07:
Wide, quiet nighttime bedroom. The same character sits or lies in bed surrounded by stillness. The room feels spacious, dark, calm, and low-stimulation. Emphasize the absence of competing distractions.

BOTTOM RIGHT — BEAT 08:
Same bedroom. A single old embarrassing memory becomes visually dominant above the character, glowing or under a subtle spotlight while the rest of the room remains subdued. The memory itself should not look physically larger than before; it simply receives more visual emphasis because the environment is quiet.

CONSISTENCY:
Preserve the exact recurring character design from Sheet 01.
Keep the bedroom recognizable.
Keep the embarrassing memory visually compatible with later reuse.
No borders, no numbers, no narration captions.
Every quadrant must be cleanly crop-safe.
```
