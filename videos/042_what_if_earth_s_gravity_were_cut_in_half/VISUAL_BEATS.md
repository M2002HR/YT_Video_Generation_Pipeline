# Visual Beats

### Beat 1

Narration:
People would weigh half as much on Earth.

Visual:
A clay Earth park scene showing two identical people on a balance platform, one normal-weight reference silhouette and one lightly compressed scale effect with a clear upward lift feeling, surrounded by familiar trees and ground.

### Beat 2

Narration:
They could jump higher and lift heavy objects more easily.

Visual:
A clay person making a high jump beside a large stone block being lifted with ease, with soft motion arcs suggested through bent poses and floating dust.

### Beat 3

Narration:
A person could carry a large box with less effort.

Visual:
A clay person calmly carrying an oversized cardboard box across a warehouse floor, showing relaxed posture despite the box size.

### Beat 4

Narration:
Bridges and buildings would face different forces.

Visual:
A clay city scene with a bridge and tall buildings showing altered support loads through thicker beams, curved supports, and people observing from below.

### Beat 5

Narration:
Some structures might need new designs.

Visual:
A clay architect workspace with a model bridge being reshaped with stronger-looking curved supports and new forms.

### Beat 6

Narration:
Cars and machines would move differently.

Visual:
Clay vehicles traveling on a road with a truck and construction machine showing altered suspension and motion, surrounded by a soft dusty landscape.

### Beat 7

Narration:
Astronauts already experience very low gravity in space.

Visual:
A clay astronaut floating inside a spacecraft cabin with soft Earth visible through a window, showing the familiar low-gravity experience.

### Beat 8

Narration:
A half-gravity Earth could look familiar but act differently.

Visual:
A clay landscape that resembles a normal Earth park while people, trees, and vehicles show subtle unusual movement from lower gravity.

### Beat 9

Narration:
Many systems would need to adjust to lower weight.

Visual:
A clay montage-like city block scene showing transportation, buildings, and daily activity adapting with redesigned supports and machinery.

### Beat 10

Narration:
Lighter does not mean the same.

Visual:
A clay Earth scene split by composition rather than borders, with familiar landscapes on one side and subtly changed movement and structures on the other.
