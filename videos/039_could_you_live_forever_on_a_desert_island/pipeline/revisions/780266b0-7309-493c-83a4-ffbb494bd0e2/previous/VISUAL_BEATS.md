# Visual Beats

### Beat 1

Narration:
Seawater has too much salt for your body.

Visual:
Full-bleed vertical linocut close-up of a bone-white drinking cup scooping turquoise seawater at the surf line, with dense rust-orange salt crystals exaggerated around the cup rim and suspended in the water to make the salt burden immediately visible.

### Beat 2

Narration:
Your kidneys use water to remove that salt.

Visual:
Full-bleed simplified anatomical linocut cutaway of a human torso against an abstract indigo field, two kidneys prominent in bone white while translucent turquoise flow streams toward them and rust-orange salt particles funnel toward an outgoing urine stream.

### Beat 3

Narration:
Drink seawater, and you may lose more water than you gain.

Visual:
Full-bleed side-view survival figure kneeling on sun-bleached sand after drinking from a shell, with one small turquoise intake stream entering the body while a visibly larger turquoise outflow leaves toward the sand, surrounded by sparse rust-orange salt marks.

### Beat 4

Narration:
Rain can help if you collect it.

Visual:
Full-bleed high-angle linocut of a sudden tropical rain shower striking broad palm leaves angled into an improvised bone-white container, turquoise rain channels converging into the vessel beneath softened storm-gray light.

### Beat 5

Narration:
Some islands have springs or fresh groundwater.

Visual:
Full-bleed cutaway of a rocky island slope, with a clear turquoise spring emerging from shaded stone above while a second turquoise groundwater pocket sits beneath the sand and roots, both surrounded by carved indigo earth layers.

### Beat 6

Narration:
Others may have no reliable fresh water.

Visual:
Full-bleed wide shot of a low barren coral islet under hard tropical sun, ringed by turquoise ocean but showing only cracked sand, dry palms and an empty tilted container casting a sharp shadow.

### Beat 7

Narration:
Desalination can turn seawater into fresh water.

Visual:
Full-bleed three-quarter view of a compact improvised desalination still on coastal rocks, seawater heating in one vessel as translucent vapor arcs through a cooling tube and condenses into clear turquoise drops in a separate clean cup.

### Beat 8

Narration:
But it needs equipment, energy, and care.

Visual:
Full-bleed low-angle still life of the desalination setup partially dismantled beside stacked fuel, a cracked hose, tools and a carefully tended flame, with hard shadows emphasizing the amount of supporting gear and maintenance involved.

### Beat 9

Narration:
Food, injury, disease, and aging still matter.

Visual:
Full-bleed island shelter scene containing four integrated survival hazards without panels: an almost-empty food basket in the foreground, a bandaged ankle beside a walking stick, a feverish figure resting under shade with a water bowl nearby, and an elderly silhouette moving slowly near the shelter entrance.

### Beat 10

Narration:
Even perfect fresh water cannot make island life last forever.

Visual:
Full-bleed dusk linocut of a pristine vessel overflowing with clear turquoise fresh water in the foreground while an abandoned weathered shelter, spent food remnants and a long aging palm shadow recede toward a storm-gray horizon, the water abundant but the island visibly finite.
