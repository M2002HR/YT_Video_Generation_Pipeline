# Visual Beats

### Beat 1

Narration:
Seawater has too much salt for your body.

Visual:
Full-bleed retro educational illustration of a shallow tide pool evaporating beside the open ocean, with pale salt crystals crusting the rocks and concentrated brine glinting under hard tropical sun.

### Beat 2

Narration:
Your kidneys use water to remove that salt.

Visual:
Full-bleed field-guide-style anatomical cutaway of a human torso centered against a softly abstracted island backdrop, kidneys emphasized naturally as water flows through the urinary system with no labels or text.

### Beat 3

Narration:
Drink seawater, and you may lose more water than you gain.

Visual:
A weathered castaway crouches beside the surf after drinking from cupped hands, while an almost-empty freshwater gourd lies nearby and the blazing shoreline stretches behind them, emphasizing worsening dehydration rather than relief.

### Beat 4

Narration:
Rain can help if you collect it.

Visual:
During a slate-gray tropical shower, broad palm leaves and a stretched scrap of cloth funnel rainwater into a clean container set on sand, with droplets visibly converging toward the catch vessel.

### Beat 5

Narration:
Some islands have springs or fresh groundwater.

Visual:
A shaded rocky interior hollow where clear freshwater seeps naturally from a stone face into a small pool beneath dense island scrub, with the ocean only faintly visible far below.

### Beat 6

Narration:
Others may have no reliable fresh water.

Visual:
A high overhead view of a small dry island surrounded by turquoise ocean, its interior showing sun-baked scrub, bare rock, and an empty cracked rain basin with no stream or spring visible.

### Beat 7

Narration:
Desalination can turn seawater into fresh water.

Visual:
At the shoreline, a compact improvised desalination setup draws seawater through tubing and a condensation chamber, producing a small stream of clear water into a separate vessel.

### Beat 8

Narration:
But it needs equipment, energy, and care.

Visual:
A wider worksite view shows the same class of desalination system surrounded by a solar panel, fuel or power components, tools, replacement filters, and a person carefully maintaining a fouled intake near the surf.

### Beat 9

Narration:
Food, injury, disease, and aging still matter.

Visual:
A full-bleed island survival scene at dusk shows four distinct but naturally integrated pressures: sparse edible plants and an empty fishing line, a bandaged leg, a feverish resting figure under a palm shelter, and an older survivor moving slowly with a driftwood staff.

### Beat 10

Narration:
Even perfect fresh water cannot make island life last forever.

Visual:
At sunset, a pristine vessel overflowing with clear freshwater sits in the foreground while an aging, weather-beaten island camp and exhausted survivors recede toward the horizon, the abundant water visibly unable to solve every other need.
