# Visual Beats

### Beat 1

Narration:
Calories give your body energy.

Visual:
Full-bleed linocut scene of a simple island meal beside a small cooking fire, with carved sunburst-like energy rays rising from the food and fire without symbols or text.

### Beat 2

Narration:
Health also needs vitamins, minerals, protein, and fats.

Visual:
Overhead full-bleed linocut composition of a varied tropical spread containing fish, coconut, leafy greens, fruit, roots and shellfish arranged naturally on a woven mat, emphasizing diversity without labels.

### Beat 3

Narration:
Eating only a few foods may still leave nutrient gaps.

Visual:
Full-bleed linocut of three identical repeated meals set in a row on bare sand while several empty food impressions and vacant spaces surround them, visually suggesting missing variety without text.

### Beat 4

Narration:
Over time, those gaps can cause health problems.

Visual:
Full-bleed linocut still life of the same limited food supply now weathered beside a drooping island plant and a cracked, weakened wooden tool, using gradual deterioration as a non-medical metaphor for deficiency over time.

### Beat 5

Narration:
Fresh plants may add nutrients those repeated meals miss.

Visual:
Full-bleed linocut close view of hands gathering fresh edible-looking island greens and fruit from a lush patch beside the beach, with the repetitive meal supply distant and secondary.

### Beat 6

Narration:
Clean drinking water is essential every day.

Visual:
Full-bleed linocut of clear collected rainwater pouring from a broad tropical leaf into a clean coconut-shell vessel, with muddy standing water visibly separated in the distant background.

### Beat 7

Narration:
Shelter protects you from heat, rain, and storms.

Visual:
Full-bleed linocut wide shot of a sturdy palm-frond shelter on raised dry ground as hard rain slants across the beach and wind bends nearby palms, while the interior remains protected.

### Beat 8

Narration:
Injuries or infections can become dangerous without medical care.

Visual:
Full-bleed linocut close scene of an injured bare foot with a small cut being rinsed and wrapped using simple clean cloth, while a distant empty horizon underscores the absence of professional medical help.

### Beat 9

Narration:
Even perfect nutrition cannot stop normal aging.

Visual:
Full-bleed linocut of a healthy tropical tree shown with a thick, weathered trunk and fallen older leaves while fresh fruit still hangs above, using natural aging as a restrained metaphor rather than depicting illness.

### Beat 10

Narration:
Plenty of food helps, but it cannot make you live forever.

Visual:
Full-bleed linocut sunset scene of an abundant island food spread in the foreground while a long human-made trail of footprints crosses the beach and eventually disappears into the tide, conveying abundance alongside mortality without text.
