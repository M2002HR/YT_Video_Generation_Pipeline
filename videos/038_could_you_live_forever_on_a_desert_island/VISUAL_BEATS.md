# Visual Beats

### Beat 1

Narration:
Storms can rip shelters apart and ruin tools.

Visual:
Retro educational field-guide illustration of a violent island squall tearing an improvised palm-frond shelter open while a wooden tool rack collapses and simple survival tools scatter across wet sand, framed by salt-stained parchment edges.

### Beat 2

Narration:
Strong sun and heat can harm your body.

Visual:
Sun-bleached field-guide illustration of a lone island survivor crouching in sparse palm shade with flushed, exhausted posture, a nearly vertical tropical sun casting a short hard shadow across pale sand.

### Beat 3

Narration:
A bad injury can become dangerous without medical care.

Visual:
Weathered field-guide illustration of an anonymous survivor seated beside a rocky tide pool, supporting an injured ankle wrapped in an improvised cloth bandage while a dropped walking stick lies nearby; the injury is serious but entirely non-graphic.

### Beat 4

Narration:
Infection can become serious without treatment.

Visual:
Retro survival-field-guide illustration focused on a forearm with a small cleanly bandaged scrape showing subtle redness around its edge, while clean water and fresh cloth sit nearby but no medicine is available; non-graphic and medically restrained.

### Beat 5

Narration:
Storms can spoil food or cut off fresh water.

Visual:
Aged coastal field-guide illustration of storm runoff flooding a small supply cache: torn food wrappings lie soaked in shallow water while an overturned covered water vessel sits beside a rain-choked freshwater collection setup.

### Beat 6

Narration:
Rescue may not arrive when you need it.

Visual:
Sun-faded field-guide illustration from behind an empty driftwood signal marker on a high beach ridge, looking toward a vast open ocean with no vessel nearby and only a tiny distant horizon silhouette too far away to help.

### Beat 7

Narration:
Even in perfect safety, your body would still age.

Visual:
Quiet retro field-guide illustration of the same anonymous human silhouette represented at three natural life stages walking along one continuous shoreline path, progressing from younger adult to visibly older adult with grayer hair and a slower posture, without labels or text.

### Beat 8

Narration:
Supplies can extend survival, but they cannot make life endless.

Visual:
Final salt-weathered field-guide still life showing orderly food, water, shelter cloth and tools arranged securely on a calm beach beside a weathered hourglass whose sand has nearly finished falling, with the open ocean beyond.
