# Visual Beats

### Beat 1

Narration:
Storms can rip shelters apart and ruin tools.

Visual:
Primitive fresco of a violent island storm tearing a branch-and-leaf shelter open while a stone knife and wooden tool are scattered into rain and wind.

### Beat 2

Narration:
Strong sun and heat can harm your body.

Visual:
Primitive cave-painting scene of a lone human silhouette crouching beneath a blazing sun pictograph on exposed pale rock, with heat-wave strokes surrounding the body.

### Beat 3

Narration:
A bad injury can become dangerous without medical care.

Visual:
Fresco close-up of an injured leg beside sharp rocks, with a simplified human silhouette gripping the wounded limb and no medical supplies anywhere in the scene.

### Beat 4

Narration:
Infection can become serious without treatment.

Visual:
Primitive diagram-like fresco showing a small wound on a forearm surrounded by expanding dark pigment rings and branching marks that suggest spreading infection.

### Beat 5

Narration:
Storms can spoil food or cut off fresh water.

Visual:
Cave-art still of rainwater flooding a primitive food cache while muddy runoff contaminates a small freshwater pool nearby.

### Beat 6

Narration:
Rescue may not arrive when you need it.

Visual:
Minimalist fresco of a tiny stranded human silhouette on a high coastal rock raising both arms toward an empty horizon with no boat or rescuer present.

### Beat 7

Narration:
Even in perfect safety, your body would still age.

Visual:
Primitive symbolic fresco of one human life shown as a single continuous silhouette sequence progressing from upright youth to bent old age beneath an unchanged calm sun.

### Beat 8

Narration:
Supplies can extend survival, but they cannot make life endless.

Visual:
Reflective cave-art composition with abundant primitive food, water vessels, shelter materials, and stone tools surrounding a long ochre path that still ends at the final aged human silhouette.
