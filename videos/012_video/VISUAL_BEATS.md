# Visual Beats

### Beat 1

Narration:
How were such immense structures planned?

Visual:
A vertical archaeological engraving of the Giza plateau with a pyramid shown as a measured architectural reconstruction: faint charcoal baseline, corner sightlines, proportional guide marks, and a few scale figures beside limestone courses, all on sun-aged papyrus with restrained ochre and desert-blue accents.

### Beat 2

Narration:
How were heavy materials moved into place?

Visual:
Engraved workers hauling a large limestone block on a wooden sledge across prepared ground, ropes visibly under tension; a faint alternative ramp contour appears as a lightly drawn hypothetical reconstruction rather than a definitive scene.

### Beat 3

Narration:
How did builders keep each side aligned?

Visual:
Top-down oblique engraving of a pyramid foundation corner with taut surveying cords, straight reference lines, simple wooden measuring implements, and carefully squared limestone edges casting sparse dusty shadows.

### Beat 4

Narration:
What tools made the work possible?

Visual:
A simple archaeological still life on papyrus: copper-era chisels, stone pounders, wooden mallets, rope, a wooden lever, and a plumb-like surveying implement arranged beside a partially worked limestone block, rendered as evidence objects rather than a labeled museum display.

### Beat 5

Narration:
How was a project this complex organized?

Visual:
Wide engraved scene linking quarry edge, hauling route, work crews, food preparation area, and worker settlement near the plateau through natural spatial flow; small groups perform different tasks without any modern hierarchy chart or readable labels.

### Beat 6

Narration:
What did the pyramids mean to the people who lived around them?

Visual:
Quiet archival-style engraving of a completed pyramid dominating the desert horizon while nearby settlement figures, procession-like silhouettes, and cultivated land occupy the foreground; symbolic elements remain restrained and non-specific rather than inventing religious claims.

### Beat 7

Narration:
Which clues are solid evidence, and which interpretations remain debated?

Visual:
A single archaeological composition with excavated limestone courses, tool marks, a sledge fragment, settlement remains, and surveying traces rendered in firm dark engraving, while possible ramp and organizational reconstructions appear only as pale, incomplete charcoal overlays fading into the papyrus.

### Beat 8

Narration:
That distinction matters, because this brief includes no source notes to check.

Visual:
Close archaeological desk-like composition without readable writing: a few evidence fragments and measured drawings sit sharply rendered at the center, while empty papyrus margins and unfinished annotation strokes surround them, emphasizing the absence of supporting source material.

### Beat 9

Narration:
So rather than turn uncertainty into a confident story, we stop where the evidence stops.

Visual:
A partially reconstructed pyramid drawing ends deliberately at a crisp boundary: confirmed masonry and excavated traces are rendered in complete engraved lines on one side, while the unverified continuation dissolves into blank papyrus with only a few faint unfinished guide strokes.
