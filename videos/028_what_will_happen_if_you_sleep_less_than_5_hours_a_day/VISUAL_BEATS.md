# Visual Beats

### Beat 1

Narration:
Attention drifts; obvious details slip past.

Visual:
Layered paper-cut close view of the Red Horned Everyman at a sorting table in an abstract subject-world workspace, looking past a clearly different apple while several nearly identical apples pass under his unfocused gaze; slate-blue fatigue layers surround his head and a faint honey highlight on the brain dims toward the edges.

### Beat 2

Narration:
Reaction time slows, making mistakes easier.

Visual:
Top-down paper-cut scene of two hands reaching toward a falling apple, with the correct catching hand lagging behind while the apple knocks into a second object; staggered cream and slate motion silhouettes show delayed response without any text or markings.

### Beat 3

Narration:
New memories become harder to store.

Visual:
Simplified cut-paper brain shown in three-quarter view as glowing honey memory tokens approach a layered hippocampus-like inner pocket, but several tokens slide off the edge instead of settling inside; the background becomes a quiet slate-blue anatomical space.

### Beat 4

Narration:
Emotional control weakens, so feelings hit harder.

Visual:
The Red Horned Everyman stands small and calm-looking in profile while an oversized layered red-amber paper wave representing emotion rises behind him; a thin honey-brain control ribbon that normally contains the wave is visibly frayed and loose.

### Beat 5

Narration:
Hunger shifts toward more calorie-dense foods.

Visual:
Low-angle paper-cut still life where simple high-calorie food silhouettes occupy the bright honey-lit foreground while lighter foods recede into muted slate layers; the host's hand enters from the side choosing the denser option without showing brands or labels.

### Beat 6

Narration:
Your immune system gets less recovery time.

Visual:
Night-themed layered paper body silhouette reclines horizontally while small shield-like immune cells repair tiny gaps across the figure; a large pale clock-shape without markings presses inward, leaving several repairs unfinished as slate-blue layers overtake the warm honey recovery glow.

### Beat 7

Narration:
Blood pressure control can worsen.

Visual:
Vertical cutaway of a simplified blood vessel with a pulsing honey-red stream pressing more strongly against narrowing paper vessel walls; a small heart sits below as the source, with layered pressure arcs expanding outward but no gauges, numbers, or labels.

### Beat 8

Narration:
Blood sugar control can worsen too.

Visual:
Paper-cut bloodstream seen as a winding river through a cream anatomical landscape, carrying amber sugar-like hexagonal shapes; several shapes remain crowded in the stream while a nearby cell-shaped paper pocket accepts fewer than expected, suggesting impaired control without labels.

### Beat 9

Narration:
Repeated short sleep is linked with heart disease and diabetes.

Visual:
A repeated row of small layered bed silhouettes recedes into the background like successive nights, converging toward two large foreground symbols: a stressed paper heart and an overloaded metabolic cell cluster, both partially darkened by accumulating slate-blue layers.

### Beat 10

Narration:
Caffeine can mask sleepiness, but cannot replace sleep.

Visual:
The Red Horned Everyman sits upright beside a plain unbranded paper cup, his eyes temporarily ringed by a small honey alertness glow while a larger translucent slate-blue sleep-debt shadow remains attached behind his body; a distant bed silhouette stays visibly separate and untouched.

### Beat 11

Narration:
Most adults need at least seven hours nightly.

Visual:
Calm wide paper-cut night scene of an adult silhouette sleeping in a bed beneath seven small unmarked moon-phase-like paper discs arranged as a purely visual rhythm, with warm honey recovery light returning across the body and slate fatigue layers receding toward the frame edges.
