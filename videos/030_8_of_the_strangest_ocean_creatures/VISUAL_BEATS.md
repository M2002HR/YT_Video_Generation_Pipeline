# Visual Beats

### Beat 1

Narration:
A barreleye fish wears a transparent dome over its head.

Visual:
Vertical monochrome line-art close-up of a barreleye fish drifting in open deep water, its transparent cranial dome outlined with a single restrained cyan accent so the structure reads instantly.

### Beat 2

Narration:
Its tubular eyes rotate upward to watch for prey above.

Visual:
Technical-drawing-style cutaway of the barreleye seen from a three-quarter underside angle, with paired tubular eyes visibly pivoted upward toward a tiny prey silhouette overhead.

### Beat 3

Narration:
A goblin shark shoots its jaws forward to snatch prey.

Visual:
Side-on line illustration of a goblin shark lunging horizontally as its jaws project far ahead of its snout toward a small fish, with sparse current lines emphasizing the sudden strike.

### Beat 4

Narration:
A vampire squid flips its webbed arms outward, revealing tiny spines.

Visual:
Front-facing vampire squid suspended against sparse deep-water marks, arms flipped outward into a broad circular web with tiny spine-like cirri picked out around the inner surface.

### Beat 5

Narration:
A leafy seadragon disappears among seaweed with leaflike camouflage.

Visual:
Tall seaweed thicket drawn in loose monoline contours with a leafy seadragon woven vertically through the fronds, its appendages deliberately echoing surrounding blades so its outline is initially hard to separate.

### Beat 6

Narration:
A giant isopod looks like an armored pill bug.

Visual:
Low-angle illustration of a giant isopod crawling across the seafloor, its overlapping segmented plates emphasized, while the Red Horned Everyman crouches at a respectful distance studying its pill-bug-like armor.

### Beat 7

Narration:
A Dumbo octopus flaps ear-like fins through the deep.

Visual:
Small Dumbo octopus shown from a high three-quarter view gliding through a broad empty water column, with its paired ear-like fins caught at opposite points of a gentle flap.

### Beat 8

Narration:
An anglerfish dangles a glowing lure in darkness.

Visual:
Anglerfish emerging from a nearly empty off-white-to-graphite implied abyss, shown in side three-quarter close-up with a tiny cyan bioluminescent lure hanging above its mouth and attracting a small prey silhouette.

### Beat 9

Narration:
A blanket octopus unfurls a huge web between its arms.

Visual:
Wide vertical illustration of a blanket octopus sweeping upward through open water as an enormous webbed membrane unfurls behind and between its arms like a flowing biological sail.
