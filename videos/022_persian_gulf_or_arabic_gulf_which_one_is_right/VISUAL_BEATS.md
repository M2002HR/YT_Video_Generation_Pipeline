# Visual Beats

Narration:
Ancient maps show the waterway between Iran and the Arabian Peninsula.

An engraved papyrus map spread across a wooden table, showing a stylized ancient waterway between two landmasses with ships and compass markings, without readable labels.

### Beat 3

Narration:

### Beat 4

Visual:
