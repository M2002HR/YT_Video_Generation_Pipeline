Why can a yawn from across the workbench make you yawn too? The answer is a reflex with a social side. Scientists call this contagious yawning. Seeing a yawn can trigger one. So can hearing one. Even thinking about yawning can do it. Your brain constantly tracks other people's faces and actions. Researchers think social attention may help prime your own yawn response. But the exact brain mechanism is still unsettled. Low oxygen is not the cause. That old idea failed experimental tests. Chimpanzees and bonobos can catch yawns too.
