Pandora didn’t actually open a box. In the oldest Greek version of the myth, it was a sealed jar. Pandora, driven by curiosity, opened it—and suffering, disease, and hardship escaped into the world. She shut it too late. Only hope remained inside. Centuries later, the jar became a “box” in popular retellings, but the warning stayed the same: one tempting action can unleash consequences you can’t put back. That’s why “opening Pandora’s box” still means starting something that suddenly becomes much bigger than you expected.
