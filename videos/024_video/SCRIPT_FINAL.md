Can a cat be alive and dead at once? I wonder, feeding chickens. So I grab the physics book beside me. In 1935, Erwin Schrödinger imagined a cat sealed in a box. Inside, a radioactive atom may decay or not. Decay triggers a mechanism that releases poison. No decay means the cat survives. Quantum theory allows both atomic possibilities in superposition. That links the cat to both possible fates. Schrödinger used this absurd scenario to expose the measurement problem. It was a thought experiment, not a real cat test.
