# Visual Beats

### Beat 1

Narration:
Takhti was born in Tehran in 1930.

Visual:
A hand-painted historical view of 1930 Tehran with a small family home, cobbled street, and a newborn-era memory atmosphere inside the sketch painting frame.

### Beat 2

Narration:
He became Iran's Olympic freestyle wrestling star.

Visual:
A young Takhti-inspired wrestler in mid-century training clothes stands in an old wrestling hall holding a rope and preparing for competition, surrounded by traditional wrestling equipment.

### Beat 3

Narration:
He won gold at the 1956 Melbourne Olympics.

Visual:
A symbolic painted Olympic memory showing Takhti on a podium receiving a gold medal beneath a soft archival arena glow, with no readable text.

### Beat 4

Narration:
He earned silver medals in Rome and Tokyo.

Visual:
Two silver medals rest beside painted travel stamps and wrestling gear, with faint silhouettes of Olympic arenas representing Rome and Tokyo.

### Beat 5

Narration:
Fans admired his humility and fair play.

Visual:
A respectful crowd of Iranian fans surrounds Takhti after a match, with gentle expressions and a humble bow replacing celebration poses.

### Beat 6

Narration:
Takhti helped opponents after fierce matches.

Visual:
Takhti offers a hand and supports a tired opponent after a wrestling bout on a simple mat, emphasizing sportsmanship.

### Beat 7

Narration:
Jahan Pahlavan means world champion.

Visual:
A painted symbolic scene of a traditional champion figure standing beneath a globe motif and wrestling belt-like cloth, representing the meaning of the title.

### Beat 8

Narration:
The title reflected respect, not only victories.

Visual:
An old wrestling hall scene where a champion is surrounded by peers rather than trophies, showing honor through relationships.

### Beat 9

Narration:
He became a symbol of Iranian sportsmanship.

Visual:
A painted national sports memory card showing Takhti's figure beside a wrestling mat, traditional Iranian motifs, and respectful athletes watching him.

### Beat 10

Narration:
His legacy still inspires athletes today.

Visual:
Modern-day young athletes in a simple training hall look at an old Takhti photograph on the wall while practicing wrestling.

### Beat 11

Narration:
A Tehran statue honors his lasting memory.

Visual:
A hand-painted Tehran street scene featuring a statue of Takhti with people quietly passing and paying respect.

### Beat 12

Narration:
People remember Takhti for character and courage.

Visual:
A final archival-style painted portrait of Takhti's calm face beside a worn wrestling rope and open memory book, emphasizing character.
