# Visual Beats

### Beat 1

Narration:
Attention starts slipping, making simple tasks harder to follow.

Visual:
In the layered anatomical storybook world, the tired protagonist stands at a barn workbench trying to sort three simple farm objects while a faint amber attention-path above his head breaks into disconnected paper segments.

### Beat 2

Narration:
Forming new memories becomes more difficult.

Visual:
A simplified paper-cut brain fills the frame as several glowing amber memory tokens travel toward a hippocampus-like region, but some fade and fall away before reaching it.

### Beat 3

Narration:
Reaction time slows, increasing the chance of mistakes and accidents.

Visual:
The protagonist reaches too late to catch a falling metal feed scoop, while a delayed amber signal travels visibly from his eye to brain to hand in an elongated paper pathway.

### Beat 4

Narration:
Your mood may become more irritable or low.

Visual:
A waist-up portrait of the protagonist sits against a softly layered blue background as the warm glow around him shrinks and dusty rose storm-like paper shapes gather near his temples and shoulders.

### Beat 5

Narration:
Hunger signals can shift toward more calorie-dense foods.

Visual:
Inside a stylized torso silhouette, two paper signal paths diverge toward food symbols, with the stronger glowing path bending toward a rich pastry and the weaker path toward a simple apple.

### Beat 6

Narration:
Repeated short nights can make blood sugar harder to control.

Visual:
A layered paper bloodstream curves through the frame as many small glucose-like amber shapes remain crowded in the vessel instead of passing smoothly into nearby simplified body cells.

### Beat 7

Narration:
Blood pressure can rise with ongoing sleep restriction.

Visual:
A vertical artery rises through the center of the image while the paper vessel wall visibly tightens inward and the flow inside presses more forcefully against its sides, marked by restrained dusty rose stress accents.

### Beat 8

Narration:
Your immune defenses may become less effective.

Visual:
Simplified cream-colored immune-cell paper figures drift sluggishly through a dark blue tissue field while several tiny invading shapes pass gaps that would normally be blocked.

### Beat 9

Narration:
Severe tiredness can trigger brief microsleeps without warning.

Visual:
The protagonist stands carrying a grain bucket when his eyes suddenly close and a small circular pool of dark paper shadow snaps around his head while the rest of the barn remains sharply lit.

### Beat 10

Narration:
During a microsleep, your brain briefly stops responding normally.

Visual:
A close side-profile head cutaway shows the protagonist's eye, brain, and incoming sound-and-light signals, with the external signals reaching the brain but ending at a temporary dark interruption zone.

### Beat 11

Narration:
One short night usually brings mostly temporary effects.

Visual:
A night-to-morning visual metaphor shows a wilted paper plant under moonlight on the left gradually standing upright again as warm morning light reaches it from the right.

### Beat 12

Narration:
But regularly sleeping under five hours can compound strain across multiple systems.

Visual:
A layered full-body silhouette of the protagonist stands centered while separate brain, heart, blood vessel, immune, and metabolic paper motifs around him all carry small dusty rose strain marks connected by one dimming amber network.
