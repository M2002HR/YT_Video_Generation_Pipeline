## Description — paste into YouTube

What does Nassim Taleb mean by a Black Swan? This Q Station Short explains the idea: a Black Swan is an event outside normal expectations, with enormous impact, that people often make look predictable in hindsight. The metaphor comes from the old European belief that all swans were white until Europeans encountered black swans in Australia. The practical lesson is that forecasts built from past observations can miss consequential surprises in markets, technology, and beyond. Taleb's response is not perfect prediction, but less dependence on fragile forecasts and more room for error. Q Station explores questions that open bigger worlds of ideas.
