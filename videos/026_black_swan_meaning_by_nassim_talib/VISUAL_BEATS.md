# Visual Beats

### Beat 1

Narration:
A Black Swan is an event outside normal expectations.

Visual:
Inside the vertical screen-printed atlas card, a neat field of small white swan silhouettes follows a calm diagonal path while one oversized soot-black swan abruptly enters from the opposite corner, breaking the expected pattern; bone-white ground, storm-blue accents, coarse matte ink grain.

### Beat 2

Narration:
Its impact is enormous.

Visual:
A single black swan lands at the center of a compact screen-printed landscape, its impact sending a vermilion fracture ring through bridges, towers, and geometric structures arranged around it; low-angle composition emphasizes scale and disruption.

### Beat 3

Narration:
Afterward, people build explanations that make it look predictable.

Visual:
A tabletop-like pale stone field shows a completed jagged path ending at a black swan; behind the event, precise soot-black arrows and technical construction lines are being retroactively drawn backward to form an apparently tidy route, with one restrained vermilion endpoint.

### Beat 4

Narration:
Hindsight hides how surprising it really was.

Visual:
A torn screen-print veil of orderly forecast lines slides over a chaotic burst beneath it, concealing most of the original vermilion rupture while leaving only one jagged edge visible; close cropped composition with broad matte shadows.

### Beat 5

Narration:
The name comes from Europe's old belief that all swans were white.

Visual:
A simplified old European lakeshore rendered as a flat natural-history scene: several white swans float beneath dark cypress-like silhouettes and pale classical shoreline shapes, arranged symmetrically as if the observed world contains only one kind of swan.

### Beat 6

Narration:
Then Europeans encountered black swans in Australia.

Visual:
An Australian shoreline cue with flat eucalyptus silhouettes and a broad water inlet; a black swan stands prominently in shallow water while two tiny soot-black observer silhouettes on the far bank face it in surprise, viewed from a wide side angle.

### Beat 7

Narration:
One observation shattered a rule that had seemed certain.

Visual:
A large white circular rule-symbol made from repeated white swan silhouettes occupies the center; one black swan punches through its edge, splitting the circle into angular fragments with a sparse vermilion crack.

### Beat 8

Narration:
Taleb says forecasts can fail the same way.

Visual:
A thin storm-blue forecast curve advances cleanly across a pale field toward a narrow expected corridor, then a black anomaly shape appears far outside it and the projected line fractures sharply before reaching the edge.

### Beat 9

Narration:
Models usually depend on what has already been observed.

Visual:
A stack of observed pebble-like data marks sits along the bottom of a technical drawing; precise geometric arcs rise only from those existing marks, forming a model whose entire structure is visibly anchored to the recorded past.

### Beat 10

Narration:
Markets can change through events those models barely considered.

Visual:
A symbolic market skyline of stacked blocks and bridge-like price structures fills the lower half; an unforeseen soot-black wedge enters from the side and causes several structures to tilt and cascade, while faint model curves remain intact in a distant corner.

### Beat 11

Narration:
Technologies can overturn assumptions just as suddenly.

Visual:
A row of sturdy mechanical gears and familiar industrial tools occupies one side of the frame while a compact abstract teal device-form drops in from above, abruptly redirecting the entire mechanism so the old gear train breaks apart along a vermilion fault.

### Beat 12

Narration:
You cannot reliably predict every Black Swan.

Visual:
A sparse field of thin probability paths spreads upward like branching tracks, but several terminate at blank edges while a lone black swan silhouette appears in an untouched region beyond all drawn paths; high overhead composition with ample pale stone space.

### Beat 13

Narration:
So reduce dependence on fragile forecasts.

Visual:
A delicate tower balanced on one thin forecast line leans precariously on the left, while on the right a lower, wider structure rests on several independent supports with no single line carrying the whole load; flat architectural screen-print metaphor.

### Beat 14

Narration:
Leave room for error before surprise arrives.

Visual:
A sturdy bridge-like system spans the frame with visibly unused gaps, spare supports, and open buffer space between structural blocks; in the distance a small black swan approaches, but the system has room to flex without breaking.
