# Visual Beats

### Beat 1

Narration:
In 1935, Schrödinger imagined a cat in a sealed box.

Visual:
A mid-1930s study rendered as an aged physics-notebook illustration, with a sealed wooden box centered on a desk and a calm stylized cat silhouette visible only through a conceptual ink cutaway.

### Beat 2

Narration:
A radioactive atom could release poison if it decayed.

Visual:
Close cutaway inside the box showing a tiny radioactive-source motif beside a detector linkage and sealed poison vial, with a single decay-like ink burst poised to trigger the mechanism.

### Beat 3

Narration:
Quantum theory can represent decay and no decay together.

Visual:
An abstract notebook diagram of the radioactive source appearing as two translucent overlapping possibilities: one quiet intact state and one softly radiating decay state, both occupying the same position.

### Beat 4

Narration:
So the cat seems alive and dead before observation.

Visual:
Inside a softly drawn box cutaway, two pale translucent cat states overlap in the same space: one alert and upright, the other peacefully still and reclining, with neither presented as the single settled outcome.

### Beat 5

Narration:
It was a challenge, not a real experiment.

Visual:
A worn notebook lies open on an otherwise ordinary 1930s desk, where the cat-box apparatus exists only as a loose conceptual ink sketch hovering within the page while the surrounding room remains plainly real and empty.

### Beat 6

Narration:
Schrödinger was challenging superposition at everyday scale.

Visual:
A familiar domestic room is drawn at a wide scale with a chair, table, lamp, and sealed box, while the box and nearby cat form carry subtle doubled translucent contours that clash visually with the solid ordinary furniture.

### Beat 7

Narration:
The paradox asks when quantum possibilities become one ordinary outcome.

Visual:
Several pale overlapping probability paths curve toward a sealed box from above, then converge at the moment its lid is opened into one crisp, ordinary cat silhouette beside the box.
