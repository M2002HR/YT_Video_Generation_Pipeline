How can one atom decide a whole cat's fate? Below the red hatch, decay may trigger a detector and start a larger chain. Schrödinger proposed it in 1935. A cat is sealed in a box. One radioactive atom may decay. A detector watches for decay. Decay can trigger a poison mechanism. Then the cat dies. No decay leaves it alive.
