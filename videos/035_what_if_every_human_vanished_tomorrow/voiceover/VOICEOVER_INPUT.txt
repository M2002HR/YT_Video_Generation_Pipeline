Unattended fires can burn unchecked. Weeds crack pavement; vines climb buildings. Rust, leaks, and frost weaken roofs. Wild animals spread through quieter towns. Satellites eventually fail or fall from orbit. Stone, glass, plastic, and monuments can outlast generations. Nature wouldn't erase us quickly; it would build over us.
