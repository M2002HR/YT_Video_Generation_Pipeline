# Visual Beats

### Beat 1

Narration:
Within hours, roads empty and power grids begin failing.

Visual:
Charcoal-and-graphite high-angle view of a deserted urban avenue with stalled vehicles below and dark utility poles receding toward a hazy skyline, while one distant transformer gives off a faint cold-amber dying glow.

### Beat 2

Narration:
Without operators, pumps stop and tunnels start flooding.

Visual:
Low interior charcoal view inside a silent underground pumping chamber, with inactive machinery looming on one side as dark water spills through a tunnel mouth and spreads across the foreground floor.

### Beat 3

Narration:
Pets trapped indoors face danger first.

Visual:
Intimate charcoal interior of a dim abandoned apartment, centered on a worried house cat beside an empty tipped water bowl near a closed doorway, with soft window haze isolating the animal.

### Beat 4

Narration:
Livestock escape fences or die without care.

Visual:
Wide charcoal rural scene of cattle pushing through a broken pasture fence into an overgrown roadside, with an abandoned barn receding behind them beneath a heavy gray sky.

### Beat 5

Narration:
Unattended fires can burn unchecked.

Visual:
Dramatic charcoal street-level view of flames consuming the upper floor of an empty building, smoke smearing into the soot-dark sky while no firefighters or people appear anywhere in the street.

### Beat 6

Narration:
Weeds crack pavement; vines climb buildings.

Visual:
Ground-level charcoal composition focused on thick weeds forcing apart fractured asphalt in the foreground while a vine-covered building facade rises vertically behind them.

### Beat 7

Narration:
Rust, leaks, and frost weaken roofs.

Visual:
Upward-looking charcoal view beneath a decaying industrial roof, where rusted beams frame a jagged leak, water stains spread across concrete, and frost has split the roof edge around a sagging section.

### Beat 8

Narration:
Wild animals spread through quieter towns.

Visual:
Long-lens charcoal view down a silent suburban street where a small group of deer walks between abandoned cars, framed by dark hedges and distant houses softened by graphite haze.

### Beat 9

Narration:
Satellites eventually fail or fall from orbit.

Visual:
Charcoal orbital scene above the curved dark Earth, with one powerless satellite tumbling at a steep angle while a few small debris fragments trail away into black graphite space.

### Beat 10

Narration:
Stone, glass, plastic, and monuments can outlast generations.

Visual:
Low wide charcoal view of a weathered stone monument standing amid collapsed modern structures, with broken glass catching pale light and tangled plastic remnants lodged among rubble and dead moss.

### Beat 11

Narration:
Nature wouldn't erase us quickly; it would build over us.

Visual:
Reflective charcoal vista of a partially collapsed city block almost engulfed by mature trees, vines, moss, and layered vegetation, with recognizable stone and concrete ruins still visible beneath the growth.
