Hand-drawn 2D, muted natural palette, over-shoulder in a maritime museum prep room. A tiny severely hunched crone in ragged moss-green cloak, white messy bun, hooked nose and long pointed fingers cranks an old geared hand drill; exposed metal gears turn, catch tooth-to-tooth with a dry click, and freeze above the wood. She narrows her large eyes, side-glances at her single black raven, tests the crank, traces the locked gears, then lifts a cloth to reveal her glass orb and turns it toward camera.
