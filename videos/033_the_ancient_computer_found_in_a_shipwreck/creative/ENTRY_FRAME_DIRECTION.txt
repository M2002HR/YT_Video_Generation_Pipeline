Over-the-shoulder museum-prep view: a hand-cranked geared drill hovers above a timber display base, its exposed metal gears locked tooth-to-tooth after a sudden bind. Beside conservation crates and nautical artifacts, a moss-green sleeve and long pointed fingers release the crank, trace the frozen gear train, then lift a glass orb from beneath folded cloth. Within its curved surface, bronze Antikythera gears, engraved astronomical dials, shipwreck fragments, and luminous orbital geometry begin to emerge.
