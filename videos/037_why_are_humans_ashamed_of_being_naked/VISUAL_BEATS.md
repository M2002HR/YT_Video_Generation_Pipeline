# Visual Beats

### Beat 1

Narration:
Families and customs teach rules about modesty.

Visual:
Mineral fresco of an ancestral family group beside a cave hearth as an elder gently draws a hide wrap around a younger figure, with simple ochre gesture marks suggesting learned imitation rather than written rules.

### Beat 2

Narration:
Those rules vary across cultures and history.

Visual:
Wide limestone fresco showing a winding procession across three ancient architectural zones, with different groups using distinct combinations of draped textile, hide, ornament, and uncovered stylized silhouettes, all rendered without text.

### Beat 3

Narration:
Chosen privacy differs from unexpected exposure.

Visual:
Top-down ochre fresco of one stylized figure calmly stepping behind a suspended hide partition while, elsewhere in the same simple scene, another figure recoils as a separate partition is suddenly pulled aside by wind.

### Beat 4

Narration:
Context can also make nudity feel sexual.

Visual:
Close mineral-fresco view of two non-explicit adult silhouettes at the edge of an intimate curtained sleeping alcove, surrounded by subtle paired ochre motifs and warmer terracotta pigment, making the setting—not anatomy—the dominant cue.

### Beat 5

Narration:
Expected staring or judgment can trigger shame.

Visual:
Low-angle charcoal-and-ochre fresco of a lone figure curling inward and covering their torso while several distant faces appear only as dark eye-like silhouettes along a cave ledge, all gazes converging toward the central person.

### Beat 6

Narration:
Galleries may present nude statues as ordinary art.

Visual:
Frontal fresco of a simplified classical nude marble statue on a stone plinth inside an ancient gallery-like colonnade, with relaxed visitors calmly passing on either side and no one reacting with alarm.

### Beat 7

Narration:
Changing booths usually signal concealment from strangers.

Visual:
Oblique mineral-fresco view down a row of primitive textile changing stalls, with one person fully hidden behind a closed hide curtain while anonymous passersby remain outside the partitioned space.

### Beat 8

Narration:
The skin stays ordinary; the social meaning changes.

Visual:
Single central human silhouette painted in neutral ochre on bare limestone while surrounding contextual symbols form a loose ring: family hearth, hide curtain, watching eyes, intimate alcove motif, statue plinth, and changing partition, emphasizing that the body itself remains unchanged.
