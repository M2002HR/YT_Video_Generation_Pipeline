Slow push-in through a quiet sculpture-gallery alcove: a nude marble figure stands openly beside a fabric changing booth, its privacy curtain still taut from being snapped shut. The crone’s long finger hangs precisely between exposed stone body and concealed living body, her restrained side-eye catching the contradiction. From beneath the same ragged moss-green sleeve, her other long fingers deliberately cradle a luminous orb, whose surface reflects matched glimpses of gallery nudity, private changing, and ordinary beach exposure.
