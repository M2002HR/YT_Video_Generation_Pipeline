# Visual Beats

### Beat 1

Narration:
In Kansas City, Disney created the Laugh-O-Grams.

Visual:
A 1920s Kansas City animation workspace with hand-drawn cartoon pages, ink bottles, drafting desks, and early animation sketches spread across a warm paper-covered table.

### Beat 2

Narration:
These early cartoons came before the famous mouse.

Visual:
A stack of early cartoon drawings and flipbook pages from a 1920s studio, with empty character sketches and no recognizable later Disney icons.

### Beat 3

Narration:
The Alice Comedies blended live action with animation.

Visual:
A vintage ink-wash illustration showing a child actor silhouette stepping into a hand-drawn animated landscape, with paper layers suggesting the mix of live action and cartoons.

### Beat 4

Narration:
Those experiments shaped Disney’s future.

Visual:
A timeline-like trail of loose animation sketches, ink marks, and evolving character drawings flowing across warm paper, symbolizing creative development.

### Beat 5

Narration:
The story began long before Mickey.

Visual:
A quiet 1920s studio shelf with early cartoon reels, sketchbooks, and unfinished animation pages illuminated by a warm lamp, emphasizing beginnings before famous characters.
