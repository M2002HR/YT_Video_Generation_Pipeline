If you grew as big as an elephant, could your legs still hold your weight? As bodies grow, weight rises faster than bone strength. Picture a human at four tons. That is fifty times your normal weight. Weight grows with volume in three directions, but bone strength only grows with bone thickness. Your weight jumps fifty times, while bone thickness only grows fourteen times. Elephants survive with legs like thick pillars. Slender human legs bend at angles.
