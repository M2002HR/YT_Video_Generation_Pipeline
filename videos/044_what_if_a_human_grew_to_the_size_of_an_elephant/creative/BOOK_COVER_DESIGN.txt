The closed cover is bound in coarse, slate-grey elephant-hide leather creased with deep natural wrinkles. At the vertical center, an embossed polished ivory columnar elephant leg stands rigid, flanking a delicate brass human femur that buckles and splinters under an engraved downward wedge. Cast bronze corner brackets sculpted like thick architectural buttresses anchor the perimeter. Across the lower third, fine hairline fractures radiate outward through crushed stone inlay, visualizing the catastrophic load failure of scaled biological proportions.
