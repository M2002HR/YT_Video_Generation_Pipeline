# Visual Beats

### Beat 1

Narration:
Picture a human at four tons.

Visual:
A colossal human figure sculpted from layered cream paper cardstock looms against a cool cyan atmospheric sky, with the small silhouette of the Newton-inspired scholar standing on a slate stone terrace in the foreground looking upward in awe at the monumental scale.

### Beat 2

Narration:
That is fifty times your normal weight.

Visual:
A massive dimensional paper balance scale tilted dramatically, where a single paper silhouette of an average human on one suspended lavender pan is outweighed by a dense stack of fifty layered cardstock human cutouts pressing heavily down onto the slate base.

### Beat 3

Narration:
Weight grows with volume in three directions,

Visual:
An expanding 3D isometric cube constructed of translucent layered cream paper unfolding outward in three distinct dimensional axes, cast in directional studio light with folded paper expansion arrows indicating three-dimensional growth.

### Beat 4

Narration:
but bone strength only grows with bone thickness.

Visual:
A clean transverse cross-sectional slice of a cylindrical leg bone sculpted in concentric layers of bone-white cardstock, highlighting the circular flat cross-sectional surface area rather than volume, illuminated by soft studio rim lighting.

### Beat 5

Narration:
Your weight jumps fifty times,

Visual:
A massive vertical stack of fifty dense paper body silhouettes pressing downward as a towering column, creating deep layered drop shadows and downward coral-orange compression arrows bearing onto a single support plane.

### Beat 6

Narration:
while bone thickness only grows fourteen times.

Visual:
Two side-by-side circular bone cross-sections cut from matte white paper: one moderately widened by fourteen layers, overlaid by a faint coral-tinted outline showing the fifty-layer thickness that would actually be required to match the weight.

### Beat 7

Narration:
Elephants survive with legs like thick pillars.

Visual:
A full-height dimensional paper sculpture of an elephant's leg showing its vertical column alignment from hip to foot, constructed like an architectural classical pillar of thick slate and warm cream cardstock with zero joint angle.

### Beat 8

Narration:
Slender human legs bend at angles.

Visual:
A layered papercraft sculpture of human leg bones shown in profile, emphasizing the slender femur and tibia meeting at an acute knee angle, casting diagonal shear lines and displaying coral-orange tension crease marks at the flexed joint.

### Beat 9

Narration:
The moment you try to stand,

Visual:
A dramatic low-angle shot of the giant human legs straining to push upward from a crouch on a cracked slate foundation, paper muscle layers taut and intense coral-orange stress lines radiating across the bent knee joints.

### Beat 10

Narration:
your leg bones snap under the load.

Visual:
A dramatic mechanical failure rendered in paper: the slender bone-white cardstock femur snaps sharply along a clean jagged fracture line, buckling under the massive downward slate load with burst paper fragments and vivid coral-orange fracture edges.

### Beat 11

Narration:
To carry that weight, you need legs like tree trunks.

Visual:
The Newton-inspired scholar stands on a layered slate terrace next to a towering human silhouette reimagined with massive, flaring tree-trunk legs rooted securely into the ground, crafted from textured warm cream and slate cardstock against a serene pale sky.
