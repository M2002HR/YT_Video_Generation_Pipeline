Sorting workshop tools, I found Disney's animation story starts before Mickey. So I pull down a book: Kansas City. Disney's earliest Kansas City cartoons were the Laugh-O-Grams. They came years before Mickey. Next came the Alice Comedies. Those blended live action with animation. Steamboat Willie was a milestone, not the start.
