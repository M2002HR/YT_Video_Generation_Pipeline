# Visual Beats

### Beat 1

Narration:
Inside, tiny connected units pass signals from one layer to the next.

Visual:
A clean vertical technical illustration of three staggered layers of simple circular nodes connected by thin geometric lines, with a few pale-cyan signal pulses visibly progressing from the lower input layer upward through the middle layer toward the final layer; deep navy matte background, restrained luminous edges, no labels or interface panels.

### Beat 2

Narration:
Each connection can carry a little more or less influence.

Visual:
Close technical view of several node-to-node connections in the same layered network, with line thickness and restrained glow intensity varying clearly from faint thin links to stronger bright links while the nodes remain uniform and uncluttered.

### Beat 3

Narration:
At first, the network’s output may be far off.

Visual:
The protagonist stands beside the network diagram in crisp technical-illustration form, watching an input pattern travel through uneven weak pathways and emerge as a visibly mismatched abstract output shape compared with a nearby simple target shape; comparison is purely visual with no text or UI frames.

### Beat 4

Narration:
During training, feedback adjusts those connections again and again.

Visual:
A simplified network fills the vertical frame while a clean looping feedback path arcs from the output side back toward earlier layers; several connection lines visibly shift between thinner and thicker states along the loop, with restrained amber highlights marking the adjustments.

### Beat 5

Narration:
Those adjustments repeat, making some pathways matter more than others.

Visual:
The same network after repeated training, now with a few coherent pathways rendered as stronger luminous cyan routes while many alternative links remain faint graphite lines, creating an obvious hierarchy of important versus less-important connections.

### Beat 6

Narration:
Layer by layer, the network gets better at recognizing patterns.

Visual:
A vertical progression through successive network layers where a scattered cluster of simple input marks becomes increasingly organized into a clean repeating geometric pattern as signals move upward, with each layer extracting more structure without any readable labels.

### Beat 7

Narration:
Give it new input, and those learned patterns help shape an output.

Visual:
The protagonist introduces a fresh abstract input cluster at the left-lower side of a familiar trained network; luminous pathways selectively activate through the layers and converge into a clear organized output symbol at the upper-right, all within one simple vertical composition.

### Beat 8

Narration:
That basic loop powers many modern AI systems.

Visual:
A central simplified neural-network diagram glows softly while several restrained branching paths radiate outward toward abstract modern data destinations, implying one shared underlying mechanism powering many systems; no devices, labels, dashboards, or readable text.

### Beat 9

Narration:
They can work with text, images, sound, and other kinds of data.

Visual:
The protagonist stands at the base of one clean network while four distinct abstract input motifs flow toward it: short line-like text marks without readable words, a simple framed image pictogram, a waveform-like sound symbol, and a neutral cluster of generic data shapes; the network unifies them into the same luminous layered structure.
